A direct point of reference

Start with the right source.

VMA Community values clarity in both education and communication.

The contact information on this page is provided so visitors can reach the appropriate public channels without relying on reposted details or unofficial profiles. When asking a question, include enough context to identify the topic—such as educational content, QuantCore AI, public website information or a general community inquiry. Do not send sensitive credentials, private keys, passwords or confidential account information by ordinary email.

Response expectations

Thoughtful communication starts with clear scope.

VMA Community contact channels are intended for general information, educational questions and public website matters. Response timing may vary with inquiry volume and the complexity of the request. Messages that require individualized legal, tax or investment advice may need to be directed to an appropriately qualified professional who can review the complete circumstances.
